What this counts

"Core controls" are counted from a dated primary source per aircraft class — not X-Plane's full bindable-command list. X-Plane 12 does not publish its own consolidated per-aircraft-class control-count reference, so the counts here come from the X-Plane 12 Desktop Manual's own worked examples for flight-control button/axis assignment (pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, toe brakes, flaps, and — for the 737-800 only — landing gear) plus its full "Using the Autopilot" section for the airliner's autopilot/FMS panel. "Slots" means physical buttons, axes, and hats on the controller, counted from the manufacturer's own product page. One physical input covers one control.

This tool reports counts. It does not tell you a controller is required, sufficient, or the best choice for X-Plane — that judgment is yours.

Controllers and aircraft classes covered

Controllers: Thrustmaster T.16000M FCS, T.16000M FCS HOTAS, T.Flight HOTAS X, T.16000M FCS Space Sim Duo. Aircraft classes: GA Single-Engine (Cessna 172SP), Airliner/Jet (Boeing 737-800) — both X-Plane 12 default aircraft.

A helicopter class (Robinson R22) is not included this pass — the Desktop Manual's "Flying Helicopters" section could not be read in full within this build's research budget, and an honest smaller tool beats a guessed-at one. Honeycomb Alpha, Logitech X56, and VKB Gladiator were researched and dropped for the same reason: no current manufacturer page states a clean total count.
