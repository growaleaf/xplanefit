What this counts

"Core controls" are counted from a dated primary source per aircraft class — not X-Plane's full bindable-command list. "Slots" means physical buttons, axes, and hats on the controller, counted from the manufacturer's own product page. One physical input covers one control.

This tool reports counts. It does not tell you a controller is required, sufficient, or the best choice for X-Plane — that judgment is yours.
